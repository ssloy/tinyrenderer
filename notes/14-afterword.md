# Afterword（后记）

Computer graphics does not end with the techniques we’ve explored here, far from it. What we covered are just a handful of tools in a vast and ever-expanding toolbox. There are countless other tricks, variants, and styles waiting to be discovered, tweaked, or reinvented. And perhaps the most exciting part is this: many of the most brilliant ideas in graphics have not yet been invented. They’re out there, waiting for curious minds (maybe yours!) to stumble upon them. So treat what you’ve learned not as a conclusion, but as a launchpad for your own experiments, accidents, and breakthroughs.

> 计算机图形学并不止于我们在这里探讨过的那些技术，差得远呢。我们所讲到的，不过是浩瀚且不断扩张的工具箱里寥寥几件工具。还有数不清的其他技巧、变体和风格，等着被发现、调整或重新发明。而最激动人心的或许是这一点：图形学中许多最精彩的想法至今还没有被发明出来。它们就在那里，等着好奇的头脑（说不定就是你！）去偶然撞见。所以，别把学到的东西当作终点，而要把它当作发射台——从这里出发，去做你自己的实验，去犯错，去取得突破。

When I first published the original version of this course more than ten years ago, I had no idea what would follow. To my surprise, it spread far and wide — across countries, classrooms, labs, and late-night coding sessions. Along the way there were bugs across the globe (and yes, a fair number of tears too!), and my inbox filled with literally thousands of questions, suggestions, and stories.

> 十多年前，当我第一次发布这门课程的原始版本时，我完全不知道后面会发生什么。令我惊讶的是，它传播得又广又远——跨越了不同的国家、课堂、实验室和深夜写码的时光。这一路上，世界各地都有 bug 出没（没错，也少不了大把眼泪！），我的收件箱里塞满了数以千计的问题、建议和故事。

![Image 1](https://haqr.eu/bugs.jpg)

Responding to all those messages was sometimes overwhelming, but more than anything, it was tremendously rewarding. I had the chance to see how different people learned, struggled, and created with the material. It reminded me that teaching is a living process: every bug fixed, every clarification added, and every diagram redrawn came out of real human interaction.

> 逐一回复这些消息有时让人应接不暇，但最重要的是，这件事带给我的回报难以估量。我有机会看到不同的人如何用这份材料学习、挣扎与创造。它提醒我，教学是一个有生命的过程：每一个修复的 bug、每一处补充的说明、每一幅重画的示意图，都源自真实的人际交流。

Working on this second edition has been just as much fun — perhaps even more, because I can now build on all those years of shared experience. My hope is that this new version will continue the same spirit: practical, curious, and maybe even a little playful.

> 创作第二版同样乐趣十足——甚至更有趣，因为现在我能站在这些年共同积累的经验之上继续构建。我希望这个新版本能延续同样的精神：务实、好奇，甚至可以带点玩心。

So, to everyone who has read, written, coded, cursed, or smiled along with this course: thank you. You’ve made it a joy to write, and I can’t wait to see what you’ll do with it next. Send me screenshots!

> 所以，致每一位读过、写过、敲过代码、吐槽过，或会心一笑过的读者：谢谢你们。是你们让写作成为一件乐事，我已经迫不及待想看到你们接下来拿它做出什么了。把截图发给我！
